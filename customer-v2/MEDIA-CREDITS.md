## Interactive curtains

`hero-living-room-open.webp` is a built-in imagegen edit of the licensed Caroline Badran hero photograph. It opens the curtains to a garden while matching the room. The live demo only reveals its window regions and gathers photographed fabric panels; the original room and furniture remain fixed. This is illustrative lifestyle imagery, not a SmartHome Cairns installation or a product recording.

Prompt: Open both floor-to-ceiling sheer curtain banks, gather fabric at the edges and reveal tall glazing with a green garden. Preserve camera, framing, 3:2 aspect, architecture, sofa, cushions, tables, chandelier, TV wall, shelves and rug. No text, UI, people or added interior objects.

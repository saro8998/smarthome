BEFORE WE TALK

A few builder
questions.

Can smart-home technology be an optional upgrade?+

Yes. We can help define a simple option or several levels around your home designs and buyer budgets. Device selection, inclusions and pricing are agreed before you offer the option.

When should we bring you into a project?+

Early planning gives us more room to consider controls, network coverage, device locations and any wiring needs with your team. We can also discuss a project already underway and work through what is practical.

How do you fit in with our existing trades?+

We focus on the smart-home plan, device integration, configuration and homeowner guidance. We coordinate requirements with your nominated trades and agree on responsibilities and timing for each project.

Can we start with one home or a display home?+

Absolutely. A single project can be a useful place to choose an experience, agree on the scope and see how it fits your build and handover process.

What does it cost, and who supports the homeowner?+

We quote devices and setup for the project rather than applying one price to every build. Homeowner handover and any ongoing support are discussed as part of the scope, so everyone knows what is included.

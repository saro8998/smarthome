A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS.

Wondering
about something?

We’ll help make sense of the details.

Can I start with just one room?

Absolutely. Start with a useful upgrade, such as lighting or AC control, and add more as your needs grow. We can help you choose devices that fit your longer-term plans.

Can you use devices I already have?

Often, yes. Tell us the brands and model numbers you own. We’ll check compatibility before recommending how to connect them or whether anything needs replacing.

Will everything need an internet connection?

It depends on the devices and setup. Many Home Assistant automations can run locally, while remote access, some voice assistants and cloud-connected devices need internet. We’ll explain what works offline in your proposed system.

Are devices included in the package price?

The listed prices cover setup services. Devices are quoted separately, so you can choose what suits your property and budget. We confirm the full scope and cost before you go ahead.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We set up your chosen controls and routines, then walk you through how to use them. The goal is to make everyday life simpler for everyone in your household.

## Existing business media

The living room, phone-control, door-sensor and dashboard imagery is reused from this repository's `images/` directory, with optimised WebP copies. Lighting, climate, garage and bedtime demonstration clips are reused from the repository root. Posters are extracted from those clips.

`assets/home-tour.mp4` is a silent 32-second edit of those four clips, in that order, with benefit captions. `scripts/build-tour.py` reproduces the edit. The original source files remain unchanged.

All media is presented as inspiration for possible smart-home setups. Images of devices do not assert a partnership, endorsement, guaranteed support for every model or completed client work.
